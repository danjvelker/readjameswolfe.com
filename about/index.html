About James Wolfe

James writes on grace, patience, and the ordinary shape of the Christian life. He lives and works in a quiet Midwestern town, publishes on Substack twice monthly, reads often, and is always searching for sticks that might be swords.

Mission

This site exists to help weary Christians find comfort and confidence in Christ — through writing that takes the ordinary, undramatic parts of faith seriously.

Currently

Writing weekly, working on a first book, and slowly building out a public archive of quotations that have shaped his thinking.
